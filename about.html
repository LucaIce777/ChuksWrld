About ChuksWRLD Logistics

A UK removals and logistics company built on one simple belief: moving day should not be the hardest part of your week.

Who we are

We move it. You relax.

ChuksWRLD Logistics is a UK removals and delivery company serving homes, students and businesses across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. We handle everything from a single marketplace purchase to a full family house move — and we do it with the same care every time.

What started as one van and a determination to do right by every customer has grown into a trusted logistics service with a simple promise at its heart: you should not have to lift a finger. Where many companies stop at the kerb, our team helps load and unload. Where others quote vaguely, we give you a clear price up front. And where a move could be stressful, we make it calm, organised and genuinely straightforward.

Our work is deliberately broad because our customers' needs are. We move three-bedroom houses and studio flats. We collect a wardrobe, a sofa or a single chair. We pick up IKEA and B&Q orders, Facebook Marketplace bargains and eBay or Gumtree finds. We transport student belongings at the start and end of term, and we get luggage to the airport on time. Local runs, long-distance routes and multi-trip jobs are all part of a normal week.

ChuksWRLD Logistics is a limited company registered in England and Wales with Companies House (company number 17263579), directed by Chukwudi Ernest Onuorah — a logistics professional with a Master of Science in Logistics and Supply Chain Management from Sheffield Hallam University. That academic grounding, combined with hands-on experience, is why our operations are planned properly: the right van, the right crew, the right route, and a price that makes sense.

Behind every booking is a straightforward commitment — turn up when we say we will, protect what you own, deliver when we promise, and treat your belongings as carefully as we would our own. Prices start from £40 and every quote is free and without obligation. Whether it is one box or an entire home, we move it, and you relax.

What drives us

Our vision and our mission

Two statements that shape every decision we make — from the vans we run to the way we answer the phone.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted independent removals and logistics company in the United Kingdom — known not for being the biggest, but for being the one people recommend to their family, friends and neighbours. We want a reputation built entirely on reliability: a company that turns up on time, handles every item with care, and delivers exactly what was promised, in every nation of the UK.

We are working towards a future where booking a move is as simple and stress-free as booking a taxi, and where ChuksWRLD Logistics is the first name that comes to mind when someone in England, Scotland, Wales or Northern Ireland needs something moved properly.

Our Mission

To make moving simple, affordable and genuinely stress-free for homes, students and businesses across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. We do this by delivering every item safely and on time, communicating clearly at every stage, and standing behind honest pricing from £40 with free, no-obligation quotes.

Every mission decision comes back to one promise: we move it, you relax. That means planning each job properly, helping load and unload, protecting your belongings in transit, and treating customers the way we would want our own family treated.

Leadership

Meet the founder

The person behind every carefully planned move.

Chukwudi Ernest Onuorah — Founder & Director

Chukwudi founded ChuksWRLD Logistics with a clear vision: to build a removals and logistics company that people trust completely, because it does exactly what it promises.

He holds a Master of Science (MSc) in Logistics and Supply Chain Management from Sheffield Hallam University, giving him a strong academic foundation in route planning, inventory movement, operational efficiency and customer-focused supply chain design. That knowledge is applied directly to the daily running of ChuksWRLD Logistics — from how a van is loaded to how a long-distance route is scheduled so a customer is never left waiting.

Just as importantly, Chukwudi is hands-on. He still works on moves, meets customers face to face and personally reviews the details of each booking. That combination of formal expertise and genuine care for people is the standard the whole team is held to.
