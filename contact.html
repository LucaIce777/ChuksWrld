Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

Prices start from £40 for a single item run, and every quote is free with no obligation. The final price depends on distance, load size, access and timing.

Do you help load and unload?

Yes — your items, our care. We can help with loading, unloading or both, even if you already have your own transport.

How far do you travel?

We cover England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, including long-distance routes. Cross-water deliveries are arranged on request.

Can you collect from a store or a seller?

Absolutely. IKEA, B&Q and other store collections, plus Facebook Marketplace, eBay and Gumtree pickups are all everyday jobs for us.

How quickly will you reply?

WhatsApp is fastest and we usually reply within the hour during working hours. Email is answered the same or next working day.
