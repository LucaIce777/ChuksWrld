Our services

Seven simple ways we help homes, students and businesses move things across the UK — plus load and unload support whenever you need an extra pair of hands.

What we do

Pick the service that fits your move

Every job starts with a free quote, and everything includes careful handling, sensible protection and a friendly team who turn up on time.

🏠 House Moves

Full or part moves, big or small. Whether you are moving a one-bedroom flat across town or a family home to the other end of the country, we plan the load properly so nothing gets damaged and nothing gets left behind.

  • Full house moves and part-load moves
  • Furniture wrapped and protected in transit
  • Multiple trips arranged if you need them
  • Flats, houses, shared accommodation and student halls
Free quote

📦 Single Item Collection & Delivery

From a chair to a wardrobe. Bought one thing and no way to get it home? We collect from the address you give us and deliver it straight through your door.

  • Sofas, wardrobes, beds, fridges, treadmills and more
  • Same-day and next-day options where available
  • Photo updates so you know it is on the way
From £40

🛒 IKEA, B&Q & Store Collections

We collect, you relax. Flat-pack furniture, garden sets, white goods and click-and-collect orders picked up from the store and delivered to your door.

  • IKEA, B&Q, Argos, Dunelm, Currys and similar stores
  • Long or bulky items handled with care
  • Collection reference and receipt treated carefully
Free quote

📱 Facebook Marketplace Collections

We collect and deliver safely. Found a bargain on Facebook Marketplace? We deal with the seller, collect the item and bring it to you — no van hire needed.

  • We liaise with the seller so you do not have to
  • Items checked and protected before transit
  • Local and long-distance collection routes
From £40

🛍️ eBay & Gumtree Pickups

Fast, reliable and secure. We collect your purchase from the seller and hand it over to you in the same condition it left them.

  • Careful handling of fragile and bulky items
  • Flexible pickup windows that suit sellers
  • Ideal for furniture, bikes, electronics and appliances
From £40

🎓 Student Moves

Affordable moves for students. Moving into halls, out of halls, or between houses at the end of term — we keep it simple and keep it cheap.

  • Term-time, end-of-year and summer moves
  • Shared loads available to split the cost
  • Handy for Sheffield, Manchester and other university cities
Student friendly

On time, every time. Get your cases and boxes to the airport before you fly, or have them brought home afterwards. Stress-free luggage logistics.

  • Ideal for families, groups and long-haul travellers
  • Timed around your flight, with early starts available
